# Getting Started with Your DATS 6401 Project

## Welcome! 

This repository is your complete project workspace. Everything is organized and ready to use.

---

## First Steps (Do This Now!)

### 1. Customize the README
Open `README.md` and fill in:
- Your research question
- Your name/team members
- Your contact information
- Update the date

### 2. Find Your Datasets
Start searching for datasets that interest you:
- Browse data.gov, census.gov, kaggle.com
- Think about topics you care about
- Make sure you can find TWO compatible datasets
- Download them to `data/raw/` folder

### 3. Document Your Datasets
Open `data/raw/data_sources.md` and fill it out completely:
- Who collected the data?
- Why was it collected?
- What variables does it contain?
- What are its limitations?

## Common Questions

**Q: Where do I put my datasets?**
A: Save original files to `data/raw/`, cleaned files to `data/processed/`

**Q: How do I export charts from Tableau?**
A: Worksheet → Export → Image, save to `docs/figures/`

**Q: What format for the Tableau file?**
A: Must be .twbx (packaged workbook) - File → Save As, choose Packaged Workbook

**Q: How long should this take?**
A: Plan for 35-45 hours total spread across 3-4 weeks

**Q: Can I work in a team?**
A: Check with your instructor - if yes, fill out the Contributions section

**Q: What if I can't find good datasets?**
A: Ask your instructor for suggestions, check the data sources listed in WORKFLOW_GUIDE.md
